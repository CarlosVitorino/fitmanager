# FitManager

Small studio and independent coach owners pay for a narrow tool that handles three pack types with expiry, consumption-vs-expiry visualization, automatic reminders, and member self-service plan management — fixing documented complaints like disappearing credits, no backdating in payment reports, and members forced to email a business to manage their plan.

- **Start here.** One studio's credit lifecycle running end to end: members hold packs of the three types, book themselves into slots, credits consume automatically on the class date, packs drain closest-to-expiry first, and the owner sees a consumption-versus-expiry view with reminders firing in-app. Payment records can be manual entries at first; the proof is whether a real owner trusts the counts more than their spreadsheet.
- **Stop if.** nothing at this point
