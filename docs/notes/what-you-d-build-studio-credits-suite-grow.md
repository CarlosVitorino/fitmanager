# What you'd build: Studio Credits Suite (Grow)

A WeChat mini-program plus admin dashboard for studios, handling booking, packs, reminders, member self-service, and online pack purchases.

From the candidate "Class-credit booking and plan management for small studios", shaped against 0 products and 2 people who asked.

**For:** Small fitness studios that want one tool for member side and staff side

**From the evidence:** StudyAppt; parthbuilds-community/FitMart

Adding payments and an admin dashboard moves it toward the full fitness platform B1 asked for, and opens it to studios that sell packs online. Payments in a WeChat mini-program is real work with real compliance weight, so build this only after v1 shows studios paying. No demand figure exists to justify it today.

**Borrows:** Nothing to borrow: no competing products were recorded, so every feature below traces to the two people who asked.

**Why this size:** reach 5/10, edge 10/10, effort 9/10, proven; net value 3.0.
No figures anywhere in the research; 5 is the ceiling allowed without one, and it assumes the broader studio market B1 targets. Keeps WeChat mini-program form, which no product has and the plan names no must-have. 8 features: 1 small, 6 medium, 1 large. 8 of 8 features were asked for by someone on file.
